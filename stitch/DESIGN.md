## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for emergency, residential, and commercial plumbing services. The visual tone balances immediate crisis relief with rock-solid, licensed tradesman credibility. It rejects generic contractor tropes—such as harsh drop shadows, high-vis neon accents, and artificial gradients—in favor of an architectural, editorial aesthetic rooted in understated craftsmanship.

The brand's aesthetic blends **Modern Utilitarian** clarity with **Heritage Practicality**. A deep, heritage forest green acts as the primary anchor, conveying precision, stability, and institutional trust. This is balanced by warm, limestone off-white canvas tones that soften the interface, reducing the clinical anxiety experienced during urgent domestic crises (such as burst pipes or water heater failures).

The target audience encompasses distressed homeowners requiring rapid emergency triage, as well as deliberate commercial property managers scheduling preventive repiping projects. Visual cues emphasize decisive action: click-to-call mechanics are permanently persistent, typography is resolute and legible from arm's length on mobile devices, and transactional forms resemble structured, reassuring service logs.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate optical hierarchy calibrated for emergency triage and clear task completion.

- **Primary Deep Forest (#004741):** The operational core. Used for main brand headers, footers, primary CTA button fills, authoritative section titles, and key interface anchors.
- **Hover / Active Green (#003631):** A compressed, deep tone for interactive states on primary buttons and high-priority navigations.
- **Interactive Light Green (#025952):** Used for focused borders, subtle active states, and decorative line rules.
- **Primary Canvas Warm Cream (#F0EDE4):** The default background surface for the entire application. It provides an organic, tactile warmth that prevents optical fatigue and differentiates the brand from stark, disposable directory sites.
- **Crisp Surface White (#FFFFFF):** Reserved exclusively for interactive cards, booking modules, emergency forms, and service cards to create crisp, high-contrast focal points against the cream canvas.
- **Text Neutral Dark (#14221F):** An ultra-dense, charcoal-forest neutral delivering optimal WCAG AAA contrast against cream and white surfaces while maintaining harmony with the deep green brand hue.
- **Text Muted (#4F615D):** A controlled slate-sage for supportive body text, timestamps, input placeholders, and metadata tags.
- **Urgent Badge / Amber Accent (#C86D24):** A warm, high-visibility amber accent. Reserved strictly for 24/7 availability pills, dispatch status markers, emergency dispatch indicators, and critical callouts.
- **Subtle Surface Border (`rgba(0, 71, 65, 0.12)`):** Structural hairline boundary used across all cards, header partitions, and form inputs to eliminate reliance on heavy shadows.

## Typography

Typography relies uniformly on **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Its wide apertures, geometric underpinnings, and crisp character rendering deliver exceptional optical authority.

- **Headlines:** Display and Hero headings employ `700` and `800` weights with negative letter-spacing to produce confident, commanding statements that immediately orient users during home emergencies.
- **Numerics:** Phone numbers, service arrival countdowns, and dispatch pricing utilize bold weights (`700`) with tabular numeric spacing to guarantee zero jitter during layout transitions.
- **Body & Forms:** Body copy is typeset at `16px` with a generous `1.5` line-height ratio in `#14221F`, preventing fatigue and facilitating scanning for key certifications, service warranties, and transparent pricing.
- **Micro-Labels & Badges:** Use `label-sm` and `label-md` with slight positive tracking (`0.02em` to `0.05em`) in uppercase or title-case to maintain instant readability against dense emergency-triage backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system bounded by an authoritative maximum content container of `1280px`. 

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and a minimum outer margin of `2rem` (`32px`). Content sections leverage ample breathing room (`space-2xl` / `64px` vertical padding) to preserve clarity between dense service offerings.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters and `1.5rem` outer canvas margins. Multi-column cards compress into 2-column matrices.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1rem` canvas margins. All service lists, quote modules, and trust matrices collapse into single-column flows, strictly preserving a `48px` vertical clearance for thumb-driven taps.
- **Sticky Infrastructure:** Vertical spacing reserves a fixed `44px` offset for the utility announcement bar, `72px` for the global header navigation on desktop, and a fixed `68px` bottom margin on mobile devices to prevent content overlap with the sticky call-to-action tray.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained without gaudy dropshadows or heavy blur overlays. Depth is generated through **tonal stacking** complemented by low-opacity, structural boundaries.

- **Base Ground:** Warm Cream canvas (`#F0EDE4`), flat, matte, and non-reflective.
- **Tier 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) sitting directly on Cream, outlined by a crisp hairline border of `1px solid rgba(0, 71, 65, 0.12)`. No heavy ambient shadows; rely purely on tonal separation.
- **Tier 2 (Hero Form Card & Interactive Dropdowns):** Subtle ambient diffusion: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(20, 34, 31, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 71, 65, 0.04)`. This elevates interactive input nodes above passive content.
- **Tier 3 (Modals, Sticky Bottom Bar, & Utility Drawer):** Elevated with a structured grounding shadow: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(20, 34, 31, 0.12), 0 0 0 1px rgba(0, 71, 65, 0.08)`.
- **States:** Interactive service cards rise by `-2px` on desktop hover, transitioning the hairline border to `#025952` with a subtle elevation shift: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(0, 71, 65, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on a refined, calibrated corner curvature:

- **Base Components (`roundedness: 2` = `12px` / `0.75rem`):** Applied to primary quote cards, service grid items, input fields, interactive containers, and media frames. This 10px–14px range provides approachable modernism while preserving the industrial stability required of an engineering trade.
- **Large Containers (`rounded-lg` = `16px` / `1rem`):** Used on prominent structural hero quote cards, full-width promo panels, and map modal wrappers.
- **Pill Badges (`rounded-full` = `9999px`):** Used strictly for small micro-indicators, such as "24/7 Dispatch", "Verified Master Plumber", or status chips.
- **Divider Hairlines:** All horizontal rules and inner container dividers utilize a `1px` height matched to `rgba(0, 71, 65, 0.12)`.

## Components

### 1. Sticky Top Utility Bar & Header
- **Utility Bar:** Solid Dark Green `#004741` background, `36px` to `44px` height. Displays 24/7 availability text in warm off-white, current emergency dispatch wait time, license numbers, and a direct telephone link in bold `#F0EDE4`.
- **Main Header:** Sticky below utility bar. Background `#FFFFFF` with a `1px` bottom border of `rgba(0, 71, 65, 0.12)`. Contains company logo, service category dropdowns, service area verification, and a high-contrast Primary Phone Button (`#004741` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, phone handset icon).

### 2. Emergency Plumber Hero & Quote Form Card
- **Hero Canvas:** Left side features bold display headline ("Rapid Response Emergency Plumbing in [City]"), verified licensing badges, and a 3-bullet guarantee (30-min arrival, upfront pricing, zero mess left behind).
- **Quote Form Card:** Positioned on the right (or stacked below on mobile). Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) card, `rounded-lg` (16px), bordered in `rgba(0, 71, 65, 0.12)`. Contains step-based or direct contact fields: Emergency Service Dropdown, Street Address/ZIP, Phone Number, and a full-width high-contrast submit button labeled **"Dispatch an Emergency Plumber"**.

### 3. 16-Card Service Grid
- **Layout:** 4 columns on desktop, 2 on tablet, 1 on mobile.
- **Card Anatomy:** Crisp White `#FFFFFF` surface with `12px` corner radius and `1px` hairline border. Includes an aspect-ratio `16:10` photography slot at the top, a subtle category tag (e.g., "Drain Cleaning", "Water Heaters", "Sewer Line"), an `headline-sm` title, a two-line problem-solution description in `#4F615D`, and a dedicated "Book / Learn More" anchor with an arrow icon.
- **Hover:** Subtly shifts upward `-2px`, card border transitions to `#025952`.

### 4. Trust Bars & Process Cards
- **Trust Bar:** Cream background with monochrome client satisfaction badges, Google Guaranteed badges, 5-star aggregate review ratings, and master trade certifications rendered in `#14221F`.
- **Process Cards:** 3 or 4 step progression cards ("Call & Dispatch", "On-Site Diagnostic", "Upfront Quote", "Rapid Repair"). Features prominent numerical watermarks (01, 02, 03) in low-opacity `#004741` (8%) to convey methodical execution.

### 5. Property-Type Selector Cards
- Interactive segmented cards for selecting between "Residential", "Commercial & Industrial", and "HOA / Multi-Family". Active state applies a `#004741` border, an off-white cream fill, and an internal checkmark icon.

### 6. SEO-Friendly Accordion FAQ
- Stacked white container with inner segmented rows. Hairline borders between items. Clean toggles with plus/minus or chevron transitions. Questions styled in `headline-sm` (`#14221F`), answers in `body-md` (`#4F615D`).

### 7. Two-Column Local Map & Dispatch Section
- Left column: Detailed list of localized service zones, zip codes served, and office dispatch hours.
- Right column: Interactive map module embedded inside a `12px` rounded card with a custom map styling that matches the dark green and warm cream palette.

### 8. Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary CTA:** Deep Green `#004741` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `12px` border radius, padding `14px 28px`. Hover: `#003631`.
- **Emergency CTA:** Amber `#C86D24` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `12px` border radius, pulsating live dispatch status dot.
- **Secondary / Ghost CTA:** Pure transparent fill, `1.5px` border in `#004741`, text `#004741`. Hover: background `rgba(0, 71, 65, 0.05)`.

### 9. Inputs & Select Fields
- Height `48px` (minimum accessible touch target). Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(0, 71, 65, 0.2)`. 
- Focus state: `1px solid #004741` with a soft outer ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 71, 65, 0.1)`. Typography: `body-md` in `#14221F`.

### 10. Sticky Mobile Call Bar
- Affixed to the bottom viewport on screens `< 768px`. Height `64px`, background `#FFFFFF` with top border `1px solid rgba(0, 71, 65, 0.15)`. 
- Dual layout: Left button is "Request Quote" (secondary style), Right button is a prominent "Call Now: (XXX) XXX-XXXX" (solid `#004741` fill with an animated telephone icon for rapid conversion).
