## Brand & Style

This design system serves an elite emergency plumbing, heating, and leak-detection collective serving Brussels and Wallonia (Brussels-Capital, Brabant Wallon, Liège, Namur, Hainaut). 

The emotional imperative during a domestic plumbing crisis (fuite d'eau, canalisation bouchée, panne de chaudière en plein hiver) is severe anxiety, urgency, and vulnerability to contractor fraud. The UI must project instantaneous technical authority, radical transparency, rapid response (intervention en 30 minutes), and certified Belgian craftsmanship.

### Visual Style
The aesthetic is **Precision Technical Corporate meets Modern Tactile Utility**:
- High-contrast, clean-cut structures avoiding cluttered blue-collar tropes.
- Crisp white cards set against off-white and ice-blue backdrops, punctuated by authoritative deep navy and calming, surgical deep teal.
- High-conversion kinetic affordances: persistent, tactile emergency calling triggers, real-time availability pulses, and clear upfront pricing grids that dispel homeowner hesitation.
- Uncompromising legibility under stress and in low-light environments.

## Colors

The palette balances clinical cleanliness, certified institutional solidity, and urgent utility.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#008080` - Saphir Sarcelle / Deep Teal):** The core trust identity. Reassuring, water-associated yet distinctly elevated above generic azure plumbing tones. Used for primary CTA surfaces, trust highlights, verification markers, and active interaction states.
- **Secondary (`#10213A` - Bleu Nuit Institutionnel):** The structural anchor. Emitted across high-impact hero fields, navigation headers, primary copy, and footers. Conveys absolute authority, certified agréations, and unshakeable permanence. An auxiliary deep tone (`#062C68`) serves as a night/châssis gradient variant.
- **Tertiary / Emergency Urgent (`#E05A47` - Rouge Alerte / Intervention Rapide):** Used strictly for 24/7 urgency counters, live dispatched badge rings, and emergency callouts. Avoids radioactive reds in favor of an authoritative, high-visibility signal vermillion.
- **Neutral & Structural Foundations:**
  - `Surface Base`: `#F7F9FC` (Soft chalk grey-white; eliminates screen glare during night emergencies).
  - `Surface Tint / Ice`: `#EFF6FF` (Subtle technical glacier blue for trust badges and active form regions).
  - `Surface Raised`: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp white for interactive modular cards).
  - `Borders & Rules`: `#E4EAF1` (Precision architectural line separator).
  - `Muted Slate Text`: `#627282` (Secondary captions, certified specs, disclaimers).
  - `Slate Accent / Badges`: `#222831` (Deep slate for crisp, high-contrast badges and dark-mode pill variants).

## Typography

Typography prioritizes immediacy, geometric clarity, and zero cognitive strain. 

- **Headlines & Body (`Manrope`):** Delivers a crisp, modern geometric warmth with assertive, wide numerals and compact vertical metrics. Crucial for Belgian telephone number formatting (`02 555 00 00` / `0488 00 00 00`), hourly commitments, and clear euro pricing.
- **Functional Interface Elements (`Inter`):** Drives dense UI regions—sticky calling triggers, certified agréation badges, status pills, and form labels—ensuring absolute optical fidelity across high-stress mobile screens.
- Belgian-French typographic rules apply strictly: non-breaking thin spaces before double punctuation (`:`, `?`, `!`) and standard Belgian phone spacing formats.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined, responsive structural grid designed for rapid decision-making under stress.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stack. Content margins at `1rem` (16px) with minimum touch targets fixed at 48px. Persistent bottom sticky container pinned above safe viewport zones.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters. Forms convert to modular progressive disclosure steps.
- **Desktop (1024px - 1440px):** 12-column layout bounded to a max-width container of `1240px` centered, utilizing `2rem` (32px) gutters and `3rem` (48px) margins.
- **Hero & Emergency Flow Splitting:** On desktop, emergency conversion uses an asymmetrical 7:5 split—7 columns for service guarantee, arrival time metrics, and certified reviews; 5 columns for the floating rapid dispatch request card.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages crisp hairline boundaries combined with deep, softly diffused physical shadows. This avoids murky UI overlays and creates palpable card affordances.

### Depth Levels
1. **Level 0 (Base Surface):** 
   - Backgrounds (`#F7F9FC` or `#EFF6FF`). Zero elevation.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Modular Modules):** 
   - Pure `#FFFFFF` surface bordered with a 1px solid hairline `rgba(228, 234, 241, 0.9)`. 
   - Shadow: `0 2px 6px -1px rgba(16, 33, 58, 0.04), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(16, 33, 58, 0.02)`.
3. **Level 2 (Hover Surfaces & Quick Accordions):**
   - Border shift to `#008080` (at 40% opacity).
   - Shadow: `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(16, 33, 58, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(16, 33, 58, 0.03)`.
4. **Level 3 (Overlays, Sticky Action Bar, Floating Dispatch Modals):**
   - Background `#FFFFFF` with glassmorphic top-edge or full backing on mobile with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
   - Shadow: `0 20px 38px -6px rgba(16, 33, 58, 0.16), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(16, 33, 58, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The geometric personality features balanced, engineered radii (14px to 18px), projecting friendliness while retaining structural solidity.

- **Standard Cards & Form Panels:** `rounded-lg` (16px on desktop, 14px on mobile) to maintain clean internal spacing.
- **Buttons & Interactive Inputs:** `rounded` (8px to 10px) to retain distinct, punchy physical button clickability.
- **Trust Badges, Status Indicators, & Call Pills:** Fully rounded / pill (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for clear differentiation against content blocks.
- **Emergency Callouts & Modals:** 18px outer radius with inset nested cards matching 10px inner radii (preserving concentric border alignment).

## Components

### 1. Primary Emergency Call Button
- **Structure:** Dominant, tactile height (54px mobile, 58px desktop). 
- **Palette:** Deep Teal background `#008080`, pure white `#FFFFFF` text, paired with an animated pulse beacon (live indicator).
- **Secondary Variant:** Dark Navy background `#10213A` with `#008080` icon highlight for secondary non-critical quote requests.
- **Content:** Icon (phone handset) + primary action line ("Appel d'urgence 24/7") + immediate reassuring microcopy ("Arrivée estimée : 30 min").

### 2. Sticky Mobile Action Bar
- Pinned at screen bottom (`z-index: 99`).
- Features a split layout:
  - Left: Instant quote trigger ("Devis gratuit", secondary outline).
  - Right: High-priority immediate call ("02 800 00 00 - Urgence", filled `#008080` background with active glow).
- Elevated with Level 3 diffused upward shadow.

### 3. Trust & Certification Pills
- Height: 28px - 34px. Compact, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`).
- Background: `#EFF6FF` with `#E4EAF1` border.
- Elements: SVG badge/flag (Drapeau Belge, Agréé CSTC/Quali-Wallonie, Sans surprise de prix) followed by concise text in `label-badge`.

### 4. Verified Google Reviews Card
- Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `#E4EAF1`, radius 16px.
- Features prominent 5-star rating (`#F59E0B`), verified Google review badge, Belgian location identifier (e.g., "Uccle", "Namur Centre", "Ixelles"), and exact timestamp of intervention.

### 5. Interactive Accordion FAQ
- Collapsed: Border `#E4EAF1`, background `#FFFFFF`, chevron icon with smooth 200ms cubic-bezier rotation.
- Expanded: Active border tint `#008080` with background subtle gradient to `#F7F9FC`.
- Focuses on reassuring users regarding pricing policy ("Combien coûte un déplacement en urgence ?"), official invoice delivery for home insurance (assurance habitation), and payment methods (Bancontact / Payconiq à bord des camionnettes).

### 6. High-Conversion Fast Quote Form
- Modular, friction-free stepper:
  1. Incident type selector (Fuite d'eau, WC bouché, Chaudière en panne, Dégât des eaux).
  2. Code postal / Commune selector with auto-completion across Brussels and Wallonia.
  3. Direct phone callback validation without demanding superfluous personal data.
- Input fields use 48px height, 1px border `#E4EAF1`, focus state `#008080` with a 3px soft teal ring (`rgba(0, 128, 128, 0.15)`).
